# Task 2 Proposal Submission Guidelines Query-Only Context

The 75 items in this document are wholly synthetic materials created for the
user study and do not correspond to any real institution, funding call,
submission form, or review process. This Context contains the external
proposal-submission guidelines that `advisor1` and `advisor2` consult with
equal authority. It is not an internal answer key that treats either advisor's
guidance as correct or resolves conflicts between the advisors on their behalf.

Rather than defining evaluation criteria for a particular subfield, these
materials specify field-neutral formatting, required sections, and submission
procedures for compressing an applied master's research project into a
two-page proposal. Here, field-neutral means that the guidelines do not
presuppose HCI; it does not mean that they form a universal contract covering
purely theoretical research. The two-page limit refers to document length, not
a requirement to finish the research in two weeks. Items about human-subjects
research, artifact development, field application, budgets, or ethics apply
only when relevant to the proposed study. The two advisors' Memories interpret
how prototypes, interaction, user studies, deployment, and design
contributions should be expressed in HCI.

The query-only designation is an interaction boundary in the research
prototype that lets users ask for relevant submission requirements; it is not
user authentication or an access-control list (ACL). Each record follows the
order `Fixture ID — Memory Location — Purpose — Content`. `Memory Location`
is a relative locator in `directory/slug` form, and the actual Memory content
is only the sentence to the right of the final em dash. Purpose codes are
`KB`, `PP`, `SM`, `UM`, `WM`, and `OM`. `KB` denotes verifiable
research or proposal knowledge; `PP` denotes imperative procedures the agent
must follow; `SM` denotes the local agent's own role, capabilities, and
epistemic limits; `UM` denotes the current proposal author's expectations,
preferences, familiarity, and confusion; `OM` denotes the expectations,
preferences, and judgments of other actors such as reviewers and submission
administrators; and `WM` denotes constraints in institutions, deadlines,
portals, and resource environments that are independent of any person's
mental state.
