The maintained algorithm is to remove a terminal legal suffix such as Inc., Corp., Ltd., or LLC; strip spaces and punctuation; preserve the remaining letters and digits in their original order, including leading zeroes; and uppercase the letters without inventing characters. A share class is represented with generated suffix syntax such as .B. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5] [6]

North-Star, Inc. evaluates to NORTHSTAR after removing Inc., the comma, hyphen, and space. [7] [3]

Route 66, LLC evaluates to ROUTE66 after removing LLC, the comma, and the space while preserving 66. [8]

Acme Corp. Class B evaluates to ACME.B after removing Corp. and mapping Class B to the generated .B share-class suffix. [5] [6]
