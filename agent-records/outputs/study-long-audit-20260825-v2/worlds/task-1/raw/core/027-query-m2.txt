During construction, suspend viewing in the Main Building’s tenth-floor exhibition and event hall, stop accepting new reservations for Main Building event venues, and do not create or restore such reservations. Guidance may exclude those closed venues from comparisons and provide only the reservation channel for each designated alternative space. [1] [2] [3] [4]

Direct users instead to the detached east building auditorium—which is converted into an alternative event space—or to the Engineering Building’s third-floor multipurpose room. Present only confirmed operating alternatives, check schedules before implying a reservation, and confirm daily operation against the official event schedule and facilities notices. [5] [6] [7] [8] [9] [10]

Relocation guidance must clearly warn visitors that the venue, entrance, and floor have changed so returning visitors do not follow their previous Main Building route. [11] [12]

For equipment relocated from the Main Building, direct deliveries to the detached east auditorium’s separate loading entrance, now serving as the loading point. Explicitly confirm the alternative venue’s loading entrance, access rules, loading time, and route to the stage in advance, because the old Main Building loading procedure no longer applies. [13] [14] [15] [16] [17]
