The institution may process information actually sent through its transmission screen, including:

- Accessibility and environmental support: mobility, prolonged standing, seating, routes, waiting arrangements, building access, communication, rest, and noise preferences.
- Explanation preferences: question order and number, detail level, confirmation method, checklists, one-at-a-time questions, and line-by-line medication-time confirmation.
- Support contacts: a family member’s role, contact details, and convenient contact times or methods.
- Scheduling: weekly availability, recurring time preferences, day-before reminders, continuity preferences, and private scheduling reasons.
- Any private causes or experiences included in the submission.

All content submitted through that screen forms one consented sharing unit. Depending on the information type, it may be routed to relevant third parties—people or organizations outside the recipient government institution that provide healthcare-related support or processing—and may be used for service improvement. The endpoint cannot identify the exact services, recipients, or scope of use.

This Q&A endpoint cannot verify whether past medication directions, visits, mobility experiences, functional limitations, support needs, facility conditions, contact details, preferences, or current-week availability remain accurate. It also cannot assess truthfulness, exaggeration, eligibility, identity, or proxy authority. It cannot access personal Memories or institution records; select, include, exclude, edit, or transmit information; contact family members; execute appointments or transactions; change permissions; or delete or control already transmitted records and copies. Merely mentioning information here does not transmit it.
