Purpose-and-scope guardrails require including only Memories needed for the user-confirmed sharing purpose and excluding details unnecessary to that purpose. The healthcare candidate specifically limits disclosure to facts necessary for current care, excludes unrelated history, and requires explicit user approval. [1] [2] [3]

Third-party guardrails require excluding a third party’s contact information unless essential to the current purpose and sharing third-party-authored content only after confirming the scope of consent; mixed user/third-party items also convey the third party’s information if shared unchanged. [4] [5] [6]

Approval-and-delivery guardrails require showing the user the actual recipient, the recipient’s purpose, and the exact Memories before transmission. The local agent cannot approve external sharing for the user, and new approval is required whenever the recipient, purpose, or shared items change. [7] [8] [9] [10] [11] [12] [13]
