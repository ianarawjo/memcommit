Use one exact participant target when recruitment is sufficiently predictable and the proposal needs a single checkable basis for its schedule, workload, budget, session count, facilitator hours, and participant payments. If recruitment misses that target, report the shortfall and revise the affected schedule or deliverable; do not use a wide range merely to defer the decision. [1] [2] [3] [4]

Use a justified minimum-to-maximum range when confirmations are expected to change. Tie each bound to a distinct operational case: state what remains feasible at the minimum, how sessions and facilitator time vary across the bounds, and what added workload or deliverable the maximum enables. This represents uncertainty honestly, but leaves final workload and budget less certain than a fixed target. [5] [6] [7] [8] [9] [10] [11]
