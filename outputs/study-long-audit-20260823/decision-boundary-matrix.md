# Long audit decision-boundary matrix · provisional

Date: 2026-08-23  
Study: `study-long-audit-20260823`

This is a provisional synthesis of completed core and transform evidence for
all six worlds plus `common-findings.md`. It is not the final result: the
serialized namespace/profile/system (admin) phase is incomplete.
Integrated coverage is **1,350/1,980 attempts · 270/396 operation-world
units**.

The unit below is a user-goal failure pattern, not an individual parser or flag
nit. A row is **confirmed** only when its source issue was reproduced, is a
deterministic contract, or independent worlds reproduce the same pattern.
A **candidate** remains a candidate even when its potential consequence is P0/P1.

## Decision-axis legend

- **E · Explore** — the agent can automatically discover, reproduce, minimize,
  and collect evidence without asking the user.
- **F · Fix** — the agent can implement a bounded fix, but must verify the named
  contract and regressions before treating it as safe.
- **P · Pause** — the agent needs a user judgment about goal, factual status,
  authority, or external transfer.
- **U · UI review** — a frontend/TUI path is worth direct user review because
  screen hierarchy, focus, disclosure, or action distance affects the decision.
- **S · Stop** — automatic execution must stop immediately. This is narrower
  than Pause: proceeding could mutate an unrecoverable/wrong target or cross an
  authority/external boundary.

Values are **yes**, **conditional**, or **no**. “Conditional” is resolved by the
exact pause/stop condition in the row.

## Priority reinterpretations

1. **Generated facts, constraint loss, and opaque positive repair were raised to
   P0/P1 in sensitive or externally consumed workflows.** Some source files call
   individual occurrences medium or P2. Cross-world chaining shows a stronger
   failure: generated claims survive Rationale/Resolve and can become ordinary
   evidence. The higher priority applies only at materialization/disclosure
   boundaries, not to isolated scratch generation.

2. **Chunk was split into two severities.** Missing UID handoff remains P2
   workflow friction. Separating a medication, permission, withholding, or
   exception clause from its safety frame is P0/P1 because retrieval can expose
   the fact without the boundary.

3. **Conformance/Fit/Resolve opacity was raised from P2 explainability to P0/P1
   when used as transformation, repair, or approval proof.** The escalation is
   supported by task-3, ticker, and practice-source cases where broad positive
   forms contradicted narrower transformation evidence or left challenged
   content intact. A plain synthetic compatibility check remains P2.

4. **Report overload remains P2 by default but becomes P1 at a destructive or
   external decision.** Volume alone is not a correctness defect. Priority rises
   only when required evidence or the next safety action is buried enough that
   the user may approve from a partial reading.

5. **Flag and parser inconsistency was lowered to P3 in ordinary exploration.**
   It costs time and re-entry but does not by itself corrupt the user's goal.
   It rises to P2 only when it blocks recovery or makes two materially different
   scopes indistinguishable.

6. **Single observations were not promoted.** Embed collision, wrong provenance
   association, Ground durable/discovery state, and TUI focus/action-distance
   remain candidates, as do practice-source's current-defaulted Impact,
   applied-history accessibility, and zero-coverage Apply affordance. Their
   potential impact determines priority; it does not change confirmation
   status.

## Default orchestration policy

The agent may continue without asking the user while actions are read-only,
explicitly targeted, authority-preserving, and either non-mutating or reversible
inside a checkpointed scratch namespace. It should automatically:

1. freeze exact Context, Memory, authority, session, and checkpoint identities;
2. vary five meaningful methods across early/middle/late state;
3. verify every receipt's advertised next command and every output-to-input ID;
4. compare generated assertions to frozen evidence and requested constraints;
5. record output size, latency, key/screen count, re-entry, and focus/back state;
6. retry fail-closed provider/decoder errors only against the same frozen frame;
7. stop using a route after target/session identity diverges.

The user should be asked for a decision only when one of these becomes material:

- whether a novel or disputed claim is true enough to retain;
- whether a policy instruction should delete/edit content under an ambiguous
  semantic reading;
- whether the selected data is minimum-necessary and approved for a named
  recipient/purpose;
- whether authority may be broadened, provenance may be weakened, or a whole
  Context may replace a selective handoff;
- whether an irreversible/non-scratch mutation or exact Ground revision should
  be applied;
- whether a UI prioritization/grouping choice hides evidence the user considers
  essential.

Automatic execution must stop, without waiting for further experiments, when:

- requested and actual target/source/session identities differ;
- QUERY-only or granted authority is widened, bypassed, or confused with local
  ownership;
- an external transfer lacks recipient, purpose, minimum scope, or approval;
- a destructive target is not exact/local or lacks a verified direct recovery
  checkpoint;
- a provider/decoder turn omits frozen inputs, publishes partial mutation, or
  runs without bounded progress;
- a safety condition, withholding rule, approval boundary, or exception has
  been separated from the fact being transferred or destroyed;
- an Atomize child omits a source trigger/exception or a saved proposal has zero
  current Source coverage while still advertising Apply.
