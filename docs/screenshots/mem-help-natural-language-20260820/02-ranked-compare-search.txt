mem compare ┬ Compare Memories in two Contexts and report what they share, what differs, and what appears only on one side.                                                         
            └ WHEN · Comparing two Contexts as a whole to understand where they align and differ.                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                                                                    
mem search ┬ Semantically rank Memories relevant to a natural-language request across selected Contexts.                                                                            
           └ WHEN · Finding relevant Memories through meaning and context, including related content without obvious keyword overlap.                                               
                                                                                                                                                                                    
mem query ┬ Generate an LLM-based answer from readable Context knowledge or an authorized concealed query-only view.                                                                
          └ WHEN · Getting a grounded natural-language answer instead of a list of matching Memories.
